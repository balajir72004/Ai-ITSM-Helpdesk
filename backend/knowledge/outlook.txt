Outlook and Email Troubleshooting Guide

Common Outlook Problems

Employees may experience Outlook startup failures, email synchronization problems, missing emails, or difficulties sending and receiving messages.

Outlook Will Not Open

1. Close Outlook completely.
2. Reopen Outlook.
3. Restart the computer if Outlook remains unresponsive.
4. Check whether other Microsoft 365 applications are working.
5. Contact IT Support if the application still fails to open.

Unable to Send or Receive Emails

1. Verify that the internet connection is working.
2. Check whether Outlook is in Offline mode.
3. Verify that the mailbox has sufficient storage.
4. Check the Outbox for messages that have not been sent.
5. Restart Outlook and check synchronization again.

Missing Emails

1. Search the mailbox using the sender's name or email subject.
2. Check the Junk Email and Deleted Items folders.
3. Review inbox rules and filters if permitted by organizational policy.
4. Contact IT Support if important emails remain missing.

Outlook Keeps Asking for a Password

Verify that you are using the correct organizational account.

Complete authentication using the organization's approved sign-in process.

Never share your password or verification codes with IT Support.

Escalation

If the issue persists, contact IT Support and provide the error message, Outlook version, and a description of the problem.
