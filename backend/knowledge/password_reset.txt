Password Reset Guide

How to Reset Your Password

1. Open the organization's official password reset portal.
2. Enter your registered username or email address.
3. Complete the identity verification process.
4. Follow the instructions to create a new password.
5. Sign in using your new password.

Password Reset Troubleshooting

If the password reset link does not work, request a new link through the official portal.

If the reset email does not arrive, check the spam or junk folder.

If self-service password reset fails, contact IT Support.

Password Security

Never share your password or verification codes with anyone.

Use a strong, unique password that follows your organization's password policy.
