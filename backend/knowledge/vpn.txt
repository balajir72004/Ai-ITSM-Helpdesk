VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Common VPN Problems

Employees may experience VPN connection failures, authentication errors, or frequent disconnections.

Steps to Troubleshoot VPN Connection Issues

1. Verify that your internet connection is working.
2. Confirm that you are using the correct VPN server address.
3. Verify that your VPN username and authentication details are correct.
4. Restart the VPN application.
5. Disconnect and reconnect to the VPN.
6. Restart your computer if the problem continues.
7. Check whether your organization has announced VPN maintenance.

VPN Authentication Errors

Confirm that your account is active and your authentication method is working.

If multi-factor authentication is required, complete the verification process using your organization's approved method.

Do not share authentication codes or passwords.

VPN Connection Still Fails

Record the error message and the approximate time of the failure.

Contact IT Support if the issue persists. Provide the error message and the troubleshooting steps already attempted.
